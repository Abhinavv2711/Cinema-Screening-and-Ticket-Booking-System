R2

Review 2 (Week 11): 3NF Normalization, Constraints & SQL Views

DDL scripts, triggers, business rules, and 6 analytical views.

1. Enforced Business Rules & Triggers

✓
One Seat per Screening (Double-Booking Prevention):

Enforced by unique partial index uq_screening_seat_active on booked_seats(screening_id, seat_id) where status='Booked'.

✓
No Screen Overlap (15-Minute Turnaround Buffer):

Enforced by triggers trg_prevent_screen_overlap_insert and trg_prevent_screen_overlap_update that verify interval intersections on the same screen.

✓
Positive Quantity Constraint:

Enforced by table CHECK (quantity > 0) on concession items and order items.

✓
Cancellation Policy & Refund Limit:

Enforced by trigger trg_check_refund_limit ensuring refund ≤ original payment amount. Standard policy retains 10% cancellation charge.
